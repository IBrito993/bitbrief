BIT / BRIEF

Short daily videos about information technology.

BIT / BRIEF is a personal automation project. Once a day it reads technology news from a fixed list of established publications, picks the single story most worth watching, writes a short script in Spanish and English, and renders a vertical video for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before anything is published.

How it works

  1. Collect the day's stories from trusted technology publications via their public feeds.
  2. Select one story and write the script, crediting and linking the original source.
  3. Render a vertical video with narration and captions.
  4. Email the result to the operator for approval.
  5. Publish only after a human approves it.

Sources and attribution

Stories come from established technology publications. Each video names the publication on screen, and the original article is linked in the post description. The project summarises and links to reporting; it does not reproduce articles.
