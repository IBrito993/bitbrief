Privacy Policy

BIT / BRIEF · Last updated 1 October 2026

BIT / BRIEF ("the application") is a personal automation tool operated by a single individual for their own use. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no visitors other than its operator.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to its operator's own YouTube channel. By using the application you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's own handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can review and revoke this application's access to your Google Account at any time at Google security settings.

What the application does with Google data

The application acts only on the Google Account of its operator, who is also its developer. It requests two permissions:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send) — used solely to send the operator an approval email containing a link to a video awaiting review. The application sends email only to the operator's own address. It does not read, search, modify or delete any message in the mailbox.
  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload) — used solely to upload a finished video to the operator's own YouTube channel, after the operator has explicitly approved it. The application does not read, edit or delete existing videos, channel data, comments or analytics.

Limited Use disclosure

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, never transferred to third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train machine-learning models.

What is stored, and where

Authorisation tokens issued by Google are stored on the operator's own computer, in a local file that is excluded from version control. They are used only to perform the two actions above and are never transmitted anywhere except to Google.

The application also stores, locally, the news articles it has summarised, the scripts it has generated, and the videos it has rendered. None of this contains personal data about anyone.

What is not collected

  • No data is collected from members of the public.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising technology is used.
  • No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party.
  • The application has no users other than its operator.

Third-party services

To produce a video the application sends the text of a news story to third-party APIs for script generation and speech synthesis, and may upload finished videos to Google (YouTube), Meta (Instagram) and TikTok for publication. Only news content and the operator's own generated material is sent. No Google user data is included.

Revoking access

The operator can revoke the application's access at any time from Google Account permissions. Revoking access immediately stops all email and upload activity.

Retention and deletion of data

The application stores no personal data about anyone other than its operator, and stores nothing on a server. What exists is held locally on the operator's own computer:

  • Google authorisation tokens — deleted by removing the local token file, or by revoking access at Google security settings. Either action immediately and permanently ends the application's ability to send email or upload videos.
  • Generated scripts and rendered videos — deleted by deleting the local files. They are regenerated on demand and kept no longer than they are useful.
  • YouTube API data — the application stores no YouTube user data. It retains only the video ID returned after an upload, so it does not upload the same video twice. Deleting the local database removes those IDs.

Because there are no other users, there is no third party whose data could be retained, and therefore no deletion request process is required. The operator can delete everything at any time by removing the local files and revoking access.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
