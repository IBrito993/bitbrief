What the application does

Once a day it reads publicly available technology news, selects one story, writes an original script, renders a short vertical video, and — only after a human has reviewed and approved it — uploads that video to its operator's own channels.

Acceptable use

  • The application uploads only to channels and accounts owned by its operator.
  • It does not read, modify or delete other people's content, and does not collect data about other users.
  • It is not used to generate spam, repetitive, misleading or automated bulk content. Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before publication.
  • It does not sell, transfer or share any data obtained through Google APIs.

Content and attribution

Videos summarise reporting from established publications. Each video names the originating publication on screen and links the original article in the post description. The application does not reproduce articles in full. Narration, graphics and scripts are generated originals.
